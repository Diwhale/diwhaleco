ABOUT US

Our Story

The foundations and ambition of DIWHALE & CO.

OUR BEGINNING

Built on Independent Perspective

We established DIWHALE & CO. with a simple ambition: to develop an independent financial house grounded in thoughtful analysis, integrity and a long-term view.

Our journey begins with learning, disciplined capital allocation and the development of sound financial judgement. We intend to build capabilities carefully, growing in line with our experience, resources and applicable regulatory requirements.

DIWHALE & CO. is at an early stage of development. This website describes our direction and intended approach, not a claim of scale, track record or regulatory authorisation.
ABOUT US

Our Principles

Four principles that shape our culture, conduct and decisions.

Harmony

We value balance, collaboration and considered judgement. We seek to align ambition with responsibility and maintain constructive relationships.

Integrity

We aim to act honestly, transparently and consistently. Trust must be earned through conduct and upheld through accountability.

Loyalty

We value commitment to our responsibilities, relationships and long-term purpose, while maintaining independent judgement and ethical standards.

Industry

We believe in diligence, perseverance and continuous improvement. Meaningful work requires preparation, discipline and sustained effort.

Corporate Governance

Accountability, responsible conduct and sound decision-making.

A Responsible Foundation

We recognise that sound governance is essential to building a trusted financial institution.

As DIWHALE & CO. develops, we intend to establish appropriate policies, controls, risk oversight and compliance processes proportionate to our activities. Any regulated financial services will only be undertaken where the required authorisations and conditions are in place.
